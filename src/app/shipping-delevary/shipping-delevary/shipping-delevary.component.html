Shipping & Returns

Our commitment to delivering your style safely and swiftly across the globe.

Policy Revision: April 2026

At Cool Fashion Bazar, we strive to ensure that your shopping experience is seamless from checkout to delivery. Managed by KISMATECH SOFTWARE PVT LTD, we partner with premium logistics providers to deliver high-quality fashion across the region.

Delivery Logistics

We process all orders within 24-48 business hours. Depending on your geographical location, standard delivery typically takes between 3 to 7 working days. Our automated system will notify you as soon as your package leaves our fulfillment center.

Regional Shipping Costs

Shipping fees are calculated dynamically based on package weight and destination. We offer complimentary shipping on all orders exceeding a specific threshold — please refer to the promotional banners on our homepage for current offers.

Secure Handling

Every item is inspected and securely packaged to prevent damage during transit. If you receive a package that appears tampered with or damaged, please refuse delivery and contact our support desk immediately.
