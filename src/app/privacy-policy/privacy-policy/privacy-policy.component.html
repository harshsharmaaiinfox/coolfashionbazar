Privacy Policy

Policy Revision: April 2026

This document explains how KISMATECH SOFTWARE PVT LTD (operating as "coolfashionbazar.com") handles your information. We are committed to maintaining the trust of our visitors and customers.

Information We Collect

When you browse our catalog or make a purchase, we collect basic details such as your IP address, browser type, and purchase history. If you create an account, we store your name, email, and delivery preferences securely in our encrypted database.

How We Use Your Data

Primary use cases include processing your fashion orders, providing personalized style recommendations, and sending optional newsletters. We do not sell your personal data to third-party marketing agencies.

Third-Party Partners

To provide a full service, we share limited data with payment processors and logistics companies. These partners are strictly vetted to ensure they follow international data protection standards.

Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies to keep track of your shopping cart and session. Analytical cookies help us understand how users navigate our store so we can improve the interface and loading speeds.
