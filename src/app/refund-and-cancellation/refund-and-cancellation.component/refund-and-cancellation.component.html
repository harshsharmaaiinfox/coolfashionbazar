Financial Security

Refund & Cancellation

Our commitment to your satisfaction and financial peace of mind at Cool Fashion Bazar.

Policy Revision: April 2026

At Cool Fashion Bazar, managed by KISMATECH SOFTWARE PVT LTD, we want every shopping experience to be smooth and hassle-free. Below is our standard refund and cancellation procedure.

Order Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled directly through your user dashboard as long as the status is marked as 'Pending' or 'Processing'. Once a fashion item has been handed over to our logistics partner, cancellation is no longer possible, and the standard return policy will apply upon delivery.

Refund Mechanism

Once a returned item is verified by our quality control team, we initiate the refund within 3 business days. For credit/debit card transactions, the amount will reflect in your statement within 5-10 working days, depending on your financial institution's processing cycles.

Store Credit Option

As an alternative to a direct refund, customers can opt for 'Bazar Credits' which are added to your account instantly and can be used for future purchases across our entire catalog with no expiration date.

Exceptions

Please note that personalized or custom-made apparel, as well as items purchased during clearance 'Flash Sales', are final sale and are not eligible for cancellation or refund unless they arrive in a damaged state.
